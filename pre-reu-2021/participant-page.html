Materials

At the start of each week, we will post an updated program packet containing the materials and problems for the week here. If you have access to a printer, it may be helpful to print it out!

Packet (PDF) · Week 4 will have individual worksheets.
Abstract Vector Spaces worksheet (Tuesday 6/22).
Basel Problem worksheet (Thursday 6/24).
A helpful mathematical notation "cheat sheet."

We will update the list below with the exercises we "cover" from the packet each morning. Note that some exercises we may not have finished! The exercises listed as follow-ups may only have been given to some groups, depending on their strategy for the original problem, and some of them might not have been given to anyone. There are plenty of exercises in the packet that we will never discuss at all!

Participant Expectations

  • The program takes place 10:00am-5:00pm mountain time on weekdays June 1-25.
    There will be regular breaks, including a one-hour lunch break.
    Except for these breaks, students should be present in the room on Gather.Town and engaged in progam tasks during program hours.
  • You are encouraged, but not required, to turn your video on during small group work.
