A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

A little motion.
A lot of mathematics.

Enter a polynomial ft(z), then drag in the left plane. At each value of t, the app solves ft(z) = 0 and follows each root in a consistent color.

  1. Choose a family. Try z² − t for a first example. A counterclockwise loop around 0 exchanges its two roots.
  2. Draw around the crosses. Red × marks parameter values with repeated roots. Amber diamonds mark a vanishing leading coefficient. Paths through these points cannot be tracked as a covering.
  3. Close the loop. Use “Close loop” to join the endpoint to the start by a straight segment. The permutation sends each starting label to its ending position.
  4. Look at the braid. Switch to Braids and drag the right panel to rotate. Height records cumulative distance traveled along the parameter path.

Input: +, −, *, / by constants, ^ with nonnegative integer powers, parentheses, z, t, and i or I. Implicit multiplication such as 2z and zt is supported. Press Apply family after editing.

Numerical computation in double precision, with adaptive steps and one-to-one root matching. Degree is limited to 12 in each variable. Branch markers are computed through degree 8 in each variable; ill-conditioned families may not resolve reliably. Collision warnings stop tracking rather than assign an uncertain permutation.
